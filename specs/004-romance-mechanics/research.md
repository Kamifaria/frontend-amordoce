# Research: Romance Mechanics & Gameplay Expansion

## Decisions

### 1. In-game Phone & Calling System Balance
- **Decision**: Limiting phone calls to once per character per game-day. Calling itself does not consume Action Points (PA), but entering specific romantic dialogue branches/events during the call may consume PA if it advances the relationship status significantly.
- **Rationale**: Prevents players from spamming calls to farm affinity, while not overly punishing them just for checking in with characters.
- **Alternatives considered**: 
  - *No limits*: Allows farming and breaks game balance.
  - *Direct PA cost*: Discourages players from exploring the calling mechanic at all.

### 2. Character Choices & Thresholds
- **Decision**: Target love interests: Castiel, Lysandre, Nathaniel. 
  - Threshold of 50 affinity triggers a casual weekend invite.
  - Threshold of 75 affinity triggers a special study date invite.
  - Threshold of 90 affinity triggers a major romantic confession/event call.
- **Rationale**: Classic and familiar character roster matching existing mock/chapter data, with progression milestones that fit standard visual novel pacing.
- **Alternatives considered**:
  - *Lower thresholds*: Makes game progression feel too fast and trivializes the conquest aspect.

### 3. Dating and School Life Tips
- **Decision**: In-game "Dating Guide" interface accessible directly via the phone UI as a dedicated app (e.g., "LoveTips"). It displays unlocked hints and general advice for getting closer to each boy.
- **Rationale**: Keeps tips in-character, interactive, and gives the player a sense of progression as they unlock more hints by playing or progressing through the story.
- **Alternatives considered**:
  - *Loading screen tips*: Too random, hard to reference when needed.
  - *Helper character*: Requires heavy dialogue writing for minor utility.
